# What is Animaze Pro 22?

# 

# We greatly encourge to take a look at the latest iteration of the lifetime license: [Animaze Pro 23](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2383341/Animaze_Pro_23__Lifetime_License/)

[Animaze Pro 22](https://animaze.tv/pro22) is a one-time purchase app that will help you create your digital avatar in just a few clicks. This app is a feature-locked version of the Animaze app and it is designed to suit the needs of professional streamers that use Animaze in their streams, as it includes commercial rights!

The app is available exclusively on Steam as a DLC of the main Animaze app. 

If you have previously subscribed to Animaze Pro for at least a year, you will be able to have access to the Animaze Pro 22 app for free.

Animaze Pro 22, available for a one-time payment of **$129.99 (\~15% off original price)**, is offering:

- Everything in Animaze Plus 22
- Pro-Streamer commercial rights for streamers earning $500\+ per month/$6000 \+ per year
- Access to select Holotech Originals content sources of your choice, to modify the original avatar/background/prop to better complement your identity (up to  2 avatars, 5 accessories, 2 backgrounds and 3 emotes).

Animaze Pro 22 is only needed if you earn more than $6000 per year from Animaze-powered content (or $500 per month).

If you wish to opt for the recurrent updates, and new app features and to support the team in charge of developing the Animaze products, check out the [Animaze subscriptions!](https://www.animaze.us/pricing-individual)
