# What is Animaze Plus 22?

# We greatly encourge to take a look at the latest iteration of the lifetime license: [Animaze Plus 23](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2383340/Animaze_Plus_23__Lifetime_License/)

[Animaze Plus 22](https://animaze.tv/plus22) is a one-time purchase app that will help you create your digital avatar in just a few clicks. This app is a feature-locked version of the Animaze app and it is a strong choice for casual streamers. 

The app is available exclusively on Steam as a DLC of the main Animaze app. 

If you have previously subscribed to Animaze Plus for at least a year, you will be able to have access to the Animaze Plus 22 app for free.

**Animaze Plus 22 is available for a one time payment of $24.99 (20% off original price) and offers:**

- Remove the watermark
- Stream at 60\+ FPS
- Dedicated capture window
- Export video as MP4
- Access the entire August 22  Animaze art slate: including avatars, backgrounds, props, and more

- [80\+ customizable avatars]({{ '/faq/animazedesktop/22-avatars/' | relative_url }})
- 35\+ customizable backgrounds
- 90\+ customizable accessories
