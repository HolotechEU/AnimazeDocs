# Animaze Desktop: How do I subscribe?

Click on the coins icon in the upper right corner of the program and navigate to the “Subscriptions” tab.  

Select your desired subscription tier and term

![Find out the differences and prices of the Animaze Monthly Subscriptions]({{ '/assets/images/monthly_sub-e9f5981e.png' | relative_url }} "Animaze Monthly Subscriptions"){: width="982" height="915"}

Steam’s overlay will appear over the screen where you can authorize your purchase.
