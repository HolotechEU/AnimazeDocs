Animaze Desktop: What’s the difference between the Animaze subscriptions?

The Animaze app offers solutions for all your avatar-based communication needs, be it fun video calls, amateur or professional streaming, or business uses like education, advertising, and so much more. Choose the plan that best suits your needs. Before jumping in, remember that consumers have the option to get perpetual versions of Animaze on the Steam platform, via perpetual access DLCs. The 2024-2026 Plus DLC closely replicates the capabilities of the Plus Subscription, while the 2024-2026 DLC replicates the capabilities of the Pro Subscription. These DLC do not require periodic payments, aside from the initial purchase price, and offer all features and content released for Animaze up to the year in the DLC name.

Individual use

If you’re just starting your streaming experience or just playing around with fun video calls, you can enjoy the following features FOR FREE:

  • A ton of free fully customizable content:

    • Full-featured Character Creator

    • 30+ free avatars

    • 20+ free backgrounds

    • 30+ free accessories

    • 8+ voice effects

  • A ton of customization options for all content:

    • colors

    • textures

    • lighting setup

    • scaling

  • Ever-expanding tracking technologies set enabling you to better express yourself:

    • iPhone-based tracker - ARKit technology (best tracking there is!)

    • Best tier webcam-based tracker

    • 4 audio-based tracking technologies

    • Ultraleap hands tracking

    • Axis Neuron full-body tracking

    • VMC input

    • Mouse & Keyboard retargeting

    • gamepad triggers and hands control

    • triggerable special actions, special poses, and idle animations

    • plus a ton of procedural overrides and configuration options

  • 10+ quick scenes slots to quickly change from one setup to another

  • 4 Twitch actions that you can connect to your stream and react to your chat

  • Custom-tailored implementation and support for importing:

    • Custom Animaze Standard models

    • Live2D models (with support for Live2D animation standard and an Extended Live2D animation standard enabling enhanced expressivity)

    • VRM models (with support for VRoid animation standard and Perfect Sync)

    • Ready Player Me models

  • Session-Limited streaming  time with Animaze Virtual Camera

  • Unlimited Imports for backgrounds, avatars, and props

  • Realtime and offline video recording 

Animaze Plus subscription

If you want to upgrade your streaming game and grow your channel, you can opt for the Animaze Plus subscription, which will grant you access to the following functionalities:

  • Everything in the free subscription 

  • Remove the watermark and session time limits

  • Stream at 60 FPS 

  • Dedicated capture window

  • More export Video options 

  • Access all paid Animaze content, including avatars, backgrounds, props and more, making the content pool as big as:

    • 80+ customizable avatars

    • 35+ customizable backgrounds

    • 90+ customizable accessories

The Animaze Plus subscription costs $19.99 per year and $3.99 per month. If subscriptions are not your thing, you can also purchase a perpetual use version via the Plus 2024-2026 DLC on Steam

Animaze Pro subscription

If you are a professional streamer, you can opt for the Animaze Pro subscription to get: 

  • Everything in Animaze Plus 

  • Pro-Streamer commercial rights for streamers earning $500+ per month/$6000 + per year 

  • Early Beta Access 

Animaze Pro subscription is only needed if you already earn more than $6000 USD per year from Animaze powered content (or $500 USD per month). To be clear, if you are planning to earn $6000+ USD per year streaming with Animaze content, you do NOT need to subscribe until you actually earn around $500 a month. We’re rooting for you!

The Animaze Pro subscription costs 99.99$ per year and 9.99$ per month.  If subscriptions are not your thing, you can also purchase a perpetual use version via the Pro 2024-2026 DLC on Steam.

 

 

Business use

Looking to integrate Animaze into your business strategy? Here are the subscriptions for you: 

Animaze Small Business subscription

The Animaze Small Business subscription is designated for any business that generates less than $175,000.Includes:

  • Everything in Animaze’s free version

  • Fast track support 

  • Remove the watermark and session time limits

  • Stream at 60 FPS 

  • Dedicated capture window

  • Export video as MP4 

  • Access all paid Animaze content, including avatars, backgrounds, props, and more, making the content pool as big as:

    • 80+ avatars

    • 35+ backgrounds

    • 90+ accessories

Additionally, if you fall into this tier and use a 2D avatar, you will need to get a separate commercial license from Live2D. 

The Animaze Small Business subscription costs $499.99 per year and $139.99 per quarter. 

Animaze Business Plus subscription

The Animaze Business Plus subscription is designated for any business that generates more than $175,000 and less than 2 million.

  • Everything in Animaze’s free version

  • Fast track support 

  • Remove the watermark and session time limits

  • Stream at 60 FPS 

  • Dedicated capture window

  • Export video as MP4 

  • Access all paid Animaze content, including avatars, backgrounds, props and more, making the content pool as big as:

    • 80+ avatars

    • 35+ backgrounds

    • 90+ accessories

Additionally, if you fall into this tier and use a 2D avatar, you will need to get a separate commercial license from Live2D. 

The Animaze Business Plus subscription costs $1699.99 per year and $499.99 per quarter.
