# Animaze Desktop: Does Animaze work with glasses?

Yes, Animaze works with glasses! 

However, it’s important to note that the eyebrows and eyes are important landmarks in our facial interpretation software.  Opt for your pair of glasses with thinnest frames, and try to position them to block as little of your eyebrows as possible. Similarly, when arranging the lighting, be careful that your lenses are not reflecting too much. Reflections will confuse our tracker.  If, after all these adjustments, you feel silly, remember that with Animaze, no one will see your face!
