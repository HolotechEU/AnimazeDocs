# What is the Animaze Editor?

## Bring your Characters to Life with the **Animaze Avatar Editor**

Bring your own unique avatars creation to life with the Animaze Avatar Editor in 3 simple steps:

1. **Import your Avatar:**

   Design your avatar in Live2D or your favorite 3D modeling tool and import it to our Editor on your PC.
2. **Test your Avatar:**

   Ensure your avatar looks and behave exactly how you want with simple testing tools.
3. **Export to Animaze:**

   Export your avatar, load it onto your iPhone, and start using your avatar!
