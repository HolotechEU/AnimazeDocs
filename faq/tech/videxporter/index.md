# Animaze Desktop: How can I use the Animaze Video Exporter?

The **Animaze Video Exporte**r includes a two-step process that allows you to record your avatar’s motion-capture performance in Animaze and then export it to the resolution and framerate of your choice. Here’s how you can use it to get better videos:

First of all, open the Animaze app and click the three-dot button menu next to the **Enable Virtual Camera button \> Click Screen Capture & Record.**

![Learn how to use the Video Exporter in Animaze by FaceRig]({{ '/assets/images/video1-b6fb989d.png' | relative_url }} "Learn how to use the Video Exporter in Animaze by FaceRig"){: width="800" height="637"}

Hit the **Record Mocap Data** selection button

![Learn how to use the Video Exporter in Animaze by FaceRig]({{ '/assets/images/video2-f8286fa3.png' | relative_url }} "Learn how to use the Video Exporter in Animaze by FaceRig"){: width="800" height="709"}

Have fun recording your video and then click **Stop** when finished.

![Learn how to use the Video Exporter in Animaze by FaceRig]({{ '/assets/images/video3-f931dbdb.png' | relative_url }} "Learn how to use the Video Exporter in Animaze by FaceRig"){: width="800" height="678"}

Click **Save** to save the recorded mocap data and choose where to save the file

![Learn how to use the Video Exporter in Animaze by FaceRig]({{ '/assets/images/video4-ffd4ed1a.png' | relative_url }} "Learn how to use the Video Exporter in Animaze by FaceRig"){: width="800" height="722"}

![Learn how to use the Video Exporter in Animaze by FaceRig]({{ '/assets/images/video5-2eb68a6a.png' | relative_url }} "Learn how to use the Video Exporter in Animaze by FaceRig"){: width="800" height="730"}

After this has been completed, click the three-dot button menu and select the **Animaze Video Exporter.**

A pop-up window will appear. Browse for the saved mocap data and load it

![Learn how to use the Video Exporter in Animaze by FaceRig]({{ '/assets/images/video6-566a8782.png' | relative_url }} "Learn how to use the Video Exporter in Animaze by FaceRig"){: width="800" height="644"}

The options in the Animaze Video Exporter are:

**Quick Scene**: lets you choose between your saved Quick Scenes (pre-saved avatars and backgrounds)

**Encoder Type**: export the video as .mpeg or .mp4 (.mp4 is available only for Plus and VIP subscribers)

**Resolution**: This lets you choose the resolution of your exported video

**Framerate**: lets you select the framerate of your exported video

**Frame Blending**: Frame Blending repeats frames, but it also blends between them as needed to help smooth the motion.

**Frame by Frame toggle**: processes the mocap data on a frame by frame basis, resulting in a smoother video. However, it takes longer for the video to be processed.

**Visemes track time offset**: lets you change the delay between the sound and the avatar’s mouth movement.

Set up how you want to **export the mocap data as video** and click **Export**

![Learn how to use the Video Exporter in Animaze by FaceRig]({{ '/assets/images/video7-78613354.png' | relative_url }} "Learn how to use the Video Exporter in Animaze by FaceRig"){: width="800" height="739"}

PRO TIP: You can change the avatar and background and use the same saved mocap data to export a new video without re-recording with a different avatar and background!
