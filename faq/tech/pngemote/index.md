# Animaze Desktop: How can I use PNGs as emotes?

Load .png files to use as emotes in your streams and videos. Read on to learn more.

1. Click the Emotes icon to open the Emotes drawer menu.

![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/nqWMReMJwldR4fARB0Zu9inj39ojJVSgKx13wbwCtLrguz5ZpRxvMESTGLpzwmdir-BLmv4ho7s5zioF47A5MP5ewYesIEl3pOoKbmqx4fky7BzkRPudyoD75LqVp-U5rnL1f-VV){: width="624" height="632"}

2. Scroll down and click the “\+” button under My Emotes.
3. Browse for a .png file and double-click it to load.

![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/euzDeOKcmBaPAQm0spquJZ4zx1itxXZleVQjAJkrbWv6sfh2AFBnmgbymJ7rrzYSgEunycYcCg48M2kPgifKWQTjVRZFGLO75YO1X6luUXEbvYwaJdQIDLd3lUYPvWOJZ8AS38Dx){: width="624" height="584"}

4. Move the loaded .png file to an available slot under Preloaded Emotes by clicking the dragging the emote to the slot.
5. Click the emote or the emote hotkey number (1 through 6) to play the emote.
6. Click Save to save the changes you made.
