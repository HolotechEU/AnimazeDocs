# Animaze Desktop: How can I take screenshots and record videos in Animaze?

You can use the Animaze app to make videos and pictures of your avatar by following these steps: 

1. Click on the Streaming button found mid-bottom of the screen and select Screen Capture & Record

![]({{ '/assets/images/39_01_How_do_I_take_screenshots_and_record_videos-5a9aa8f6.png' | relative_url }}){: width="684" height="605"}

2. Going further, click the Record button or the Screenshot one, depending on what you want to create.

![]({{ '/assets/images/39_02_How_do_I_take_screenshots_and_record_videos-1d8466ef.png' | relative_url }}){: width="691" height="604"}

a. Recording

- Press the Stop button when you are done

![]({{ '/assets/images/39_03_How_do_I_take_screenshots_and_record_videos-45774f99.png' | relative_url }}){: width="512" height="409"}

- Click on either Cancel, to delete your recording, or Save to keep and save the recording

![]({{ '/assets/images/39_04_How_do_I_take_screenshots_and_record_videos-08db6112.png' | relative_url }}){: width="564" height="148"}

- Browse through your directories and choose a destination for your recording.

b. Screenshot

- Click on Save and browse through your directories to save the screenshot

![]({{ '/assets/images/39_05_How_do_I_take_screenshots_and_record_videos-2cb1e464.png' | relative_url }}){: width="680" height="536"}
