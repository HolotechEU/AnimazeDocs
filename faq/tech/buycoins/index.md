# Animaze Desktop: How can I buy more coins?

1. Click on the coins icon in the upper right corner of the program. Or, click on the 'Shop' button in the bottom menu of the program.

![Animaze FAQ \| Learn how to use Animaze by Facerig]({{ '/assets/images/unnamed_2_-571f3160.png' | relative_url }} "Animaze FAQ \| Learn how to use Animaze by Facerig"){: width="512" height="266"}

2. Navigate to the “Coin Pack” tab and select one of the available coin packs

![Animaze FAQ \| Learn how to use Animaze by Facerig]({{ '/assets/images/unnamed_3_-902ebc38.png' | relative_url }} "Animaze FAQ \| Learn how to use Animaze by Facerig"){: width="512" height="305"}

3. Steam’s overlay will appear over the screen where you will authorize your purchase through Steam
