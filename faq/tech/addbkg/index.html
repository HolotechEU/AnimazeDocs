Animaze Desktop: How can I change backgrounds?

You can change your background in Animaze by following these steps:

1. Click on the Backgrounds icon in the left menu. In the menu that will open you will have available your backgrounds, the Shop button, where you can buy new backgrounds, or the “+”, which is the import your own avatar option*

*We recommend importing custom avatars/backgrounds through the Animaze Editor, in case there are any technological discrepancies.

2. Select the Background that you want to load. Once loaded, you can take the easy way out and SAVE the selected avatar. Even moreso, if you absolutely love it, you can check the “Make a new preset” option in order to have your Animaze app open to your preferred background.
