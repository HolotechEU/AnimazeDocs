# Animaze Desktop: How can I transform a selfie into an avatar in Animaze? (Ready Player Me avatars)

In the Animaze app, you can stream and video chat as your Ready Player Me character. Go from a selfie to streaming as your avatar self in just a few clicks.

\[1\] Go to [https://animaze.readyplayer.me/](https://animaze.readyplayer.me/ "https://animaze.readyplayer.me/")

|  |
| --- |
| It is very important to use this link rather than creating the avatar through the main Ready Player Me domain, otherwise the avatar created will be lacking key features like: expression animations, special actions, special poses, idle animations and any body related tracking. |

---

\[2\] Choose a body type

![]({{ '/assets/images/rpm_step2-7adaa24a.png' | relative_url }}){: width="496" height="301"}

---

\[3\] Take a photo or pick a picture from your computer

![]({{ '/assets/images/rpm_step3-982c379b.png' | relative_url }}){: width="435" height="492"}

---

\[4\] Customize your avatar as you feel

![]({{ '/assets/images/rpm_step4-579d2a52.png' | relative_url }}){: width="1882" height="975"}

---

\[5\] Hit the **Next** button on the top right side

---

\[6\] Click **Continue without signup** on the next page

![]({{ '/assets/images/rpm_step6-4ee375ce.png' | relative_url }}){: width="635" height="405"}

---

\[7\] Copy the model’s URL and paste it into the browser’s address bar, hit Enter and wait for the model to download.

![]({{ '/assets/images/rpm_step7-412ed381.png' | relative_url }}){: width="617" height="244"}

---

\[8\] Open Animaze and navigate to the Avatars gallery

---

\[9\] Scroll to “My Avatars“ section and hit the Plus (\+) sign. This will prompt a file dialog

![]({{ '/assets/images/rpm_step9-09c53807.png' | relative_url }}){: width="364" height="434"}

---

\[10\] Find the newly downloaded avatar and select it. Wait for the import and loading to finish.

![]({{ '/assets/images/rpm_step10-19f57a36.png' | relative_url }}){: width="549" height="178"}

---

\[11\] Done

![]({{ '/assets/images/rpm_step11-4d464725.png' | relative_url }}){: width="482" height="529"}
