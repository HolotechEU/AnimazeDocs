Animaze Desktop: How can I use Stream Deck with Animaze?

If you want to use the Animaze app functionalities through your Stream Deck device, here are the steps:

Open both Animaze and Stream Deck with admin privileges. 

In the Stream Deck app, you will be able to find the  Animaze actions in the Custom category, on the right side menu. Drag and drop the actions you want available on the deck, you can choose from actions such as:

  • Change avatar
  • Change background
  • Calibrate
  • Activate emote
  • Activate special action
  • and many others! 

 If you don't have a Stream Deck device, you can also use the Stream Deck iOS application
