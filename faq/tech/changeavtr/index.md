# Animaze Desktop: How can I change avatars?

Launch the Animaze app and click on the **Avatars icon** (it's the first icon, on the left UI)

![Animaze FAQ \| Learn how to use Animaze by Facerig]({{ '/assets/images/avatars1-37f894b4.png' | relative_url }} "Animaze FAQ \| Learn how to use Animaze by Facerig"){: width="800" height="572"}  
From here you can browse either through the avatars you already own in **My Avatars**, buy new ones in the **Shop** section or **build your own character** in the **Persona** section.  
  
Once you have found the avatar you want to change to, just click on it and it will start to load on your main screen. From here, you can take a moment to go through the **avatar’s customizations like textures and colors**. Once you’re happy with how it looks, hit the **Save** button!  
  
![Animaze FAQ \| Learn how to use Animaze by Facerig]({{ '/assets/images/avatar2-eb5f1940.png' | relative_url }} "Animaze FAQ \| Learn how to use Animaze by Facerig"){: width="800" height="585"}  
Get ready to rock with your new avatar!
