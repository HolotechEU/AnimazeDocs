# What’s different about Animaze?

Animaze Desktop is an app that will help you create your digital identity in just a few clicks. Your digital identity can be a fun and cuddly pet, a futuristic nymph or even a digital depiction of your human self. You have the power to choose what you’re going to morph in, while Animaze offers you over 80 original avatars to choose from.

You can use your digital character to video call, stream, make fun creative content or even to help your business.

With Animaze, expect more cutting-edge technologies, more exciting partnerships, and a best-in-class avatar experience. As a team, we are excited to push the boundaries of Avatar Based Communication by pouring our entire focus into Animaze.

- highly customizable unique holotech studios creations \+ in-house character creator
- custom animation and retargeting stack currently supporting 4 standards suitable for already built models: Animaze, VRoid (for vrm avatars), Perfect Sync (for vrm avatars) and Ready Player Me (for RPM characters). In the future for Live2D two animation standards
- Dedicated Authoring tool (Animaze Editor), built specifically with avatars in mind and for avatars
- Integrated voice effects \+ voice skins that are AI powered and provide changing the user's voice timbre
- shading and rendering techniques that don't require rendering work for end user: cel shading, fur shading and PBR-like shading. A casual user can change those easily.
- creative way of building model combining 2D with 3D
- artists assets protection (models are bundled in encrypted files, there is no real access to sources without explicit acceptance from the artist)
