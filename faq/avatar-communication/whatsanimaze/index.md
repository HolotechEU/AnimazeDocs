# What is Animaze?

Animaze Desktop is an app that will help you create your digital identity in just a few clicks. Your digital identity can be a fun and cuddly pet, a futuristic nymph or even a digital depiction of your human self. You have the power to choose what you’re going to morph in, while Animaze offers you [over 80 original avatars to choose from]({{ '/faq/animazedesktop/holotechoriginals/' | relative_url }}).

You can use your digital character to video call, stream, make fun creative content or even to help your business.

Animaze is also a [complex avatar-based communication ecosystem of products](https://www.animaze.us/allproducts) that can provide solutions for all your digital communications needs! 

For more information, check out [www.animaze.us](http://www.animaze.us) or [drop Animaze a line](https://www.animaze.us/contact)!
