How can I use avatar based communication?

Animaze offers plenty of cool and intuitive features that will surely amp up the ways through which you can communicate with your friends, family, audience or fans! With just your phone’s web-camera you can turn into a cute lion cub, an eggplant, a penguin and even a toilet paper roll (!) - the possibilities are endless.

Animaze allows people to perform as digital characters and, through the use of the voice effects, create and invent their very own personalities and quirks.

You can share your creations with other users by uploading your videos on the Discover Feed - the place where you can inspire and be inspired.
