# What can I create with the Animaze Desktop app?

With Animaze, the sky's the limit, and the world is your oyster. Stream while playing video games, wish your siblings a happy birthday on Skype, crush that presentation on Zoom, play D&D with your friends, post a virtual class on YouTube, sing a song on Tik Tok - all as your favorite avatar.
