Animaze iPhone: I keep seeing a pop up that says I am not logged into my iCloud account!

Animaze requires an iCloud account to be logged in at all times in order to be able to restore the avatars in case the user reinstalls the application or switches phones. 

To log in with your iCloud account follow these steps:

1. Go to your phone’s settings.

2. Tap on “Sign in to your iPhone”.

3. Type in your email address in the Apple ID field.

4. Enter your password.

5. Tap on Next.


You are now logged in iCloud with your Apple ID account.
