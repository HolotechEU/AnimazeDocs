# Animaze iPhone: What are the animations and special poses?

![]({{ '/assets/images/special_animgif-34ab9a81.png' | relative_url }}){: width="320" height="552"}

In Animaze you can animate your avatar with special actions and special poses. Special poses are available for some 2D avatars. 

1. To use an animation tap on the ![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/2Bw1j6G3YgLtoM_ifiWEhxx3C-9rnhwT1rh8WhVnv33EmsiCb6HHUhIGfOledJGBzX1ETFwE-IW8x35pSi4vNvb3lVmJwtKBa1-3luGL8-s1zVMeamHQQWiQjFUKNbAQ04u-40nP){: width="32" height="33"}icon to bring up the animations menu.

1. There will be more or fewer animations available, depending on the avatar.

2. Tap on one of the available animations. 

PRO TIP: animations can also be used while recording a video.
