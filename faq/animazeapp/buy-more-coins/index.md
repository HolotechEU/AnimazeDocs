# Animaze iPhone: How do I buy more coins?

![]({{ '/assets/images/buy-more-coins_GIF-8e4d4724.gif' | relative_url }}){: width="240" height="520"}

1. To buy more coins tap on the ![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/Qaq076-C-FIN2CDHlI0bifIeJbrte1jYRFxsE-WwTTv3DPpPltP_Qk2CR8GGBJPsCIPN8VhctnI3WoQc26QGBOyn-BIuJEQc0uQEAbO9geuzAAfqy3i5mmvpoJM_ZQwOcGdcS8mb){: width="33" height="33"} icon.

1. NOTE: You can also access the Gold Shop from the Settings menu.

2. Choose a coin pack options by tapping on it.

3. Enter your password in the purchase pop-up.

1. NOTE: If you have previously made a purchase, you won’t have to re-enter your password.

4. Tap on Buy.

5. You’re all set! Your coins counter will update to reflect the purchase.
