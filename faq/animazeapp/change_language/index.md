# Animaze iPhone: How can I change the language?

![]({{ '/assets/images/change_language_GIF-07ab60d7.gif' | relative_url }}){: width="240" height="520"}

Animaze supports English, Portuguese (Brazilian Portuguese), Japanese and Russian. To change the language in Animaze you must do the following:

1. Tap on ![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/1JGIg3SAYiHYY52VxANsN3i-eSIohmgxCvJTtNB7cAxBrCcI8j-NucqfzZZtcFSAj5eunAs9_SF_7fYE2TDEto8JpYfIWRSDUHcNycqocB15ufWwCKvZpMI8h0PjfHQXXGPfXo-y){: width="32" height="32"}to access the settings menu.

2. Tap on Language.

3. Tap on the language of your choice.

4. Tap on “Done”.

5. Restart Animaze for the change to take effect.
