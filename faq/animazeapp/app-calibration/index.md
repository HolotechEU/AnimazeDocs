# Animaze iPhone: How do I calibrate Animaze?

![]({{ '/assets/images/app-calibration_GIF-a9ded238.gif' | relative_url }}){: width="240" height="520"}

**How do I calibrate for the best experience?**

1. Tap on the![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/veu9RgUNiBHvhlrEXhB_WlTS_Hr3TpDvLcXopqqVtyGEpR3fDaDrYCI883q85jRd1Q8Je387J73Bl13IBsd34kNG1rpc-I-qN1NKdwpZJjSOLI4n4E9WpbF6wdeYcSGwyzx3A6Pn){: width="42" height="42"}icon to bring up the calibration screen.

2. Position your face in the red oval area.

**PRO TIP:**

- Make sure your phone is level with your face if you are having trouble calibrating.
- When your face is correctly aligned in the oval area, the Calibrate button will turn green.

3. Tap on the Calibrate button to finalize calibration.
