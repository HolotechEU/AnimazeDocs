# Animaze iPhone: The camera feed is completely black or yellow

Most likely you denied the camera permissions. Update the permissions as follows:

1. Go to your phone’s settings.

2. Scroll down to Privacy.

3. Tap on Camera.

4. Use the toggle button to allow camera access to Animaze.

5. Return to Animaze and have fun!
