# Animaze iPhone: How do I change backgrounds?

![]({{ '/assets/images/change-background_GIF-12e66519.gif' | relative_url }}){: width="240" height="520"}

1. To choose different backgrounds, tap on the Backgrounds icon ![]({{ '/assets/images/nYkzKqH0j_qbAsXHyH2MedtktE_tsvTSZgdCNkhAby0c7nixlp80ws233iBnciCKI_4rht2AK9C7b0ZPRfMWoW5EWMUCbYH3yx3jgkM87vFuQbiWnCbMy5gRBNPtLk5-WzPjhg1n-1fa4c7cc.png' | relative_url }}){: width="39" height="39"}.

2. The Background selection menu will appear. 

3. Tap on the background thumbnail to preview a new background. 

4. Tap on the green checkmark ![]({{ '/assets/images/3LjquyWG-S6eD_czJgsun5-1N-mj1JoCvzvbh-wcWysMoaEKwH6svYq5YNvT3xHbOwQDt_m0enVXrGGF84I0LZoyWUMEBLQ4nCWBa1T04IbADQNGb682Bwfbsm_XmIiuoRFU5zUY-0402e192.png' | relative_url }}){: width="38" height="35"} to load the new background.
