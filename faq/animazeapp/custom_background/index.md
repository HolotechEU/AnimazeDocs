# Animaze iPhone: How to add my own custom background?

![]({{ '/assets/images/custom_background_GIF-d2a1ef72.gif' | relative_url }}){: width="240" height="520"}

In Animaze, you can upload your own photos to use as a background.

1. Tap on ![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/ZoCltjcDO6q_HqmX8zX5UMqUyjiW9K56KCozBQvcMw7QW5MWcT6m5uWCtbzaz3RMxOYZcZHW-PUfWG4TR1pMQKI0zfkZYKCHa2LHuXCVgJgXYPXDOrryD4SFyNZJhNCWTGWUxdRN){: width="35" height="35"}to access the background selection menu.

2. Tap on the “\+” sign under the “Imported” section.

3. You can upload an already existing photo, or take a new one with your camera.

4. Tap on “Photo Gallery” to import an already existing photo.

5. Tap on a slot to save your custom background. 

6. You can see your custom backgrounds in the “Imported” section.
