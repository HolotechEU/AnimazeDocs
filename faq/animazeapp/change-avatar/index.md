# Animaze iPhone: How do I change avatars?

![]({{ '/assets/images/freegifmaker.me_2gSKw-a43adac8.gif' | relative_url }}){: width="400" height="694"}

1. To change avatars, tap on the Avatars icon ![]({{ '/assets/images/T_FX1VJJQ4Pt9hau8oh1RDnEL1vhQu7Cnaq2GwtHC2iooI230FdsOustazyIC5sC446NTBLYqkDq1hQE5zWM77LUwq0rdICdAaQ-upOp3ivPVmAkRHO2tUsBs9IdrvzSuxpWEgpF-96ef299d.png' | relative_url }}){: width="35" height="35"}.

2. The selection menu will appear. 

3. Tap on the avatar thumbnail to preview the avatar.

4. Tap on the green checkmark ![]({{ '/assets/images/3LjquyWG-S6eD_czJgsun5-1N-mj1JoCvzvbh-wcWysMoaEKwH6svYq5YNvT3xHbOwQDt_m0enVXrGGF84I0LZoyWUMEBLQ4nCWBa1T04IbADQNGb682Bwfbsm_XmIiuoRFU5zUY-0402e192.png' | relative_url }}){: width="38" height="35"} to load the avatar

PRO TIP: If you want to load a locked avatar purchase it with coins and then download it!
