Animaze iPhone: My videos have no sound!

Check to make sure your phone is not on mute by toggling the top button on the left side of your phone. 


If that doesn’t work, you may have denied the microphone permissions. Update the permissions as follows:


1. Go to your phone’s settings.

2. Scroll down to Privacy.

3. Tap on Microphone.

4. Use the toggle button to allow microphone access to Animaze.

5. Return to Animaze and have fun!
