# Send your .avatar file to Animaze Avatar iOS app (on Windows, via Animaze Editor)

The .avatar file format acts like a container which can hold multiple types of avatar content, such as 3D/2D avatars, props, backgrounds.

Follow these steps to import:

1. First, make sure that the iphone and the computer are on the same local area network (same LAN/WiFi)

2. There are two ways to enable local connect:

1. Go to the **Tools** menu in Animaze Editor and select **Local connect**  from the dropdown menu

![Learn how to use Animaze by Facerig \| 3D Avatars ]({{ '/assets/images/1k-d28db2cb.png' | relative_url }} "Animaze by Facerig \| 3D Avatars "){: width="800" height="425"}

2. Click the **Connect** button in the upper right corner of the Animaze Editor to connect

![]({{ '/assets/images/2k-d99e1f7b.png' | relative_url }}){: width="800" height="425"}

3. **Animaze Connect** window will appear, click on the **Start** button - a **QR code** will appear

![]({{ '/assets/images/3k-2f940edf.png' | relative_url }}){: width="800" height="426"}

![]({{ '/assets/images/4k-0e6789fe.png' | relative_url }}){: width="800" height="425"}

4. Now, open the **Animaze**application on your mobile device. Click on the **Settings** wheel in the upper right corner of the main screen

![]({{ '/assets/images/5k-06d1f92b.png' | relative_url }}){: width="250" height="541"}

5. Scroll down to Animaze Editor and click **Connect**

![]({{ '/assets/images/6k-6d5b4916.png' | relative_url }}){: width="250" height="542"}

6. The rear camera is now open to point at the **QR code** and scan it

![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/sQloO5DoAzlbo23MlixGI3FRU4KUlcOO70RMw33FTIMZ-fr7mqkLpKCm775pkQtZFjVIJ2hv47nnRzsxRuMqlVArdycz-VvmCbMIP5wI-i3GlTnFfKjdovdzruCkNKsiJ6kd4Xus){: width="250" height="541"}

7. A confirmation of the connection will be received on your phone app (left image) and on the Animaze Editor (right image)

![]({{ '/assets/images/8k-616263b4.png' | relative_url }}){: width="250" height="541"}             
  
Now that you’ve connected the devices together, the next steps send the avatar to your mobile:

8. Go to the **Hierarchy** **Panel**, right click on the avatar and choose **Send to device**. This sends the avatar to your device, bundled in a \\*.avatar file format

![]({{ '/assets/images/10k-abb7dee5.png' | relative_url }}){: width="800" height="425"}

9. A pop-up will be appear on your mobile asking you to accept the file transfer

![]({{ '/assets/images/11k-7ea8d385.png' | relative_url }}){: width="250" height="542"}

10. A confirmation message of the transfer status will be displayed on both devices after you accept

![]({{ '/assets/images/12k_1-27277ec5.png' | relative_url }}){: width="250" height="542"}

11. Open the **Avatar** gallery in app and find the avatar you just sent

![]({{ '/assets/images/13k-4aef4822.png' | relative_url }}){: width="250" height="542"}                                 ![]({{ '/assets/images/14k_1-c585c161.png' | relative_url }}){: width="250" height="542"}
