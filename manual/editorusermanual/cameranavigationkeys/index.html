Camera Navigation Keys

In the viewport, you see the scene like through a camera lens and you can change the view angle (i.e., zoom, pan) by moving the mouse in combination with the left mouse button.

There are 3 camera modes easy to control, and they are detailed below:

Follow camera

Hold down left mouse button in the viewport and drag around to move the perspective smoothly, around the item.

Up/down arrows of keyboard will rotate the camera vertically around the item in scene.

Left/Right arrows of keyboard will rotate the camera horizontally around the item in scene.

Free Camera

This Camera can be used with Alt key and mouse buttons:

Alt + LMB will let you orbit around the item in scene.

Alt + MMB will let you move within the AnimazeEditor interface.

Alt + RMB will let you zoom in/out the scene.

Fly Camera

Hold down left mouse button in the viewport and drag around to move the perspective fram by frame, around the item.

Up/down arrows of keyboard will rotate the camera vertically around the item in scene.

Left/Right arrows of keyboard will rotate the camera horizontally around the item in scene.
