Introduction

Animaze allows you to embody unforgettable characters to stream, video chat, and share photos and videos on Twitch, Zoom, Instagram, TikTok, and more! Find the Animaze for free on Steam and the App Store. 

It is our mission to create a high-quality, versatile and accessible open standard.  The Animaze apps and the open .avatar format are the pillars of our mission. To help your journey in the avatar communication world, we also developed the Animaze Editor, a software that helps you bring YOUR own creations into Animaze.

We do our best to explain all of the features in Animaze and tools in the Animaze Editor. If you encounter a specific topic not covered in the documentation or just want to say hi, please contact us at support[at]animaze.us, on our Discord server, or follow us on Twitter.
