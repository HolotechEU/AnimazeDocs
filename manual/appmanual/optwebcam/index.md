# Optimizing Web Camera Tracking with Camera Angle and Ambient Light

Here are some tips and tricks to help you get the best experience out of Animaze’s tracker.

- **Light your face from the front**.
- **Avoid** placing the **light source behind** you or **partially illuminating** your face.
- **Backlighting** and **partial lighting cast shadows**, which are difficult for the Animaze tracker to interpret.

![Optimizing Animaze Web Camera Tracking]({{ '/assets/images/calibrating-794611a7.png' | relative_url }} "Optimizing Animaze Web Camera Tracking"){: width="800" height="627"}

**Too much** or **too little light** on your face will **over illuminate** or u**nder-illuminate it.** Both extremes result in an improper light distribution causing the tracker to misinterpret your features.

We recommend that you use the user feed in the top left corner to monitor your lighting. 

Frame yourself such that your **head covers about 50% of the camera’s feed** height. 

We also recommend that you use the user feed in the top left corner to **monitor your framing**. Also, make sure you’re not too far from the camera. A good rule of thumb is to stay at an **arm’s length from the camera!**

![Optimizing Animaze Web Camera Tracking]({{ '/assets/images/calibrating2-603c5b75.png' | relative_url }} "Optimizing Animaze Web Camera Tracking"){: width="800" height="629"}

If the tracking gets lost, you have to **recalibrate Animaze**. The “Calibrate” button on the bottom of the camera feed will pulsate red when the tracker needs recalibration. More on the calibration process [is available here.]({{ '/faq/animazedesktop/calibrateinanimaze/' | relative_url }})

In Settings \> General, you will find Tracking Profile Defaults. This feature has presets for the most common web camera profiles. If you have a web camera capable of recording 60 FPS, start with the High setting. For medium-quality web cameras, start with the Mid setting. For lower-end cameras, bad lighting, or experience a lot of jittering, start with the Low setting. More on the advanced settings on your camera [is available here.]({{ '/faq/animazedesktop/advancedwebcam/' | relative_url }})
