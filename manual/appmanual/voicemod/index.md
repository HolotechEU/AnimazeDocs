# Leveraging Voicemod controls in Animaze

[Voicemod](https://animaze.tv/voicemod) **is the best free voice-changing software for gamers, content creators, and Vtubers**. It allows you easily **change your voice** to have it **sound like a robot, demon, chipmunk, woman, man, or anything else you can imagine**. The technology synergizes perfectly with Animaze, **giving your digital character more depth, by complementing your visual persona with the matching voice it was missing.**

Animaze has added Voicemod controls allowing you to change Voicemod voice effects and play sound memes directly within the app, pairing them with triggering special actions, special poses, idle animations, emotes, and even quick scenes. You still need the Voicemod app to be up and running.

## Basic Usage

The first step is to **have the Voicemod app up and running**. If you haven’t got it installed you can get it from[[Voicemod](https://animaze.tv/voicemod)'s](https://www.voicemod.net/ "Voicemod") official website.  
Once you the Voicemod app is working properly, move on to **open Animaze.**

Head on to the **left-side Animaze menu** to locate and click the **Voicemod icon**. This will open up an Animaze drawer with two main tabs: **Voices and Soundboards**, mirroring all options you have available in Voicemod.

![Animaze and Voicemod]({{ '/assets/images/image-20221019-065549-323a1141.png' | relative_url }} "Animaze and Voicemod"){: width="73" height="245"}

In the **Voices tab** click on a **Voice Effect** and notice that it selected that particular item in the Voicemod app

![How to use Animaze and Voicemod]({{ '/assets/images/image-20221019-071027-c2ceebd4.png' | relative_url }} "How to use Animaze and Voicemod"){: width="597" height="685"}

The same goes for the **Soundboards tab**, click on **a sound item**.

![How to use Animaze and Voicemod]({{ '/assets/images/image-20221019-071241-0b838314.png' | relative_url }} "How to use Animaze and Voicemod"){: width="900" height="217"}

**Animaze also supports 10 [Voicemod](https://animaze.tv/voicemod) quick slots** that are keybinding. You can assign a voice effect or a sound bite to a quick slot by holding Ctrl\+Shift and clicking on the item. This action will prompt you with a Voicemod slot selection.

![How to use Animaze and Voicemod]({{ '/assets/images/image-20221019-071511-27f53e20.png' | relative_url }} "How to use Animaze and Voicemod"){: width="705" height="412"}

Once you select a slot you can trigger it with the key combo without needing to access the Voicemod drawer.

All done for primary usage.

## Tips & Tricks

## Trigger a Voicemod Sound bite and a Special Action at the same time

Change a [Voicemod](https://animaze.tv/voicemod) quick slot keybind to match a Special Action keybind by going to Settings \> Controls and scroll down to Voicemod Effect #![How to use Animaze and Voicemod]({{ '/assets/images/image-20221019-071924-242d1f5a.png' | relative_url }} "How to use Animaze and Voicemod"){: width="321" height="370"}  
  
Change the Voicemod Effect 1 keybind to Q. This will prompt a warning that it’s already used by a Special Action slot. Click **Bind Anyway**.

![How to use Animaze and Voicemod]({{ '/assets/images/image-20221019-072034-996f6be3.png' | relative_url }} "How to use Animaze and Voicemod"){: width="828" height="200"}

Open the [Voicemod](https://animaze.tv/voicemod) drawer and assign a sound bite to the first Voicemod Effect quick slot.

![How to use Animaze and Voicemod]({{ '/assets/images/image-20221019-072302-adf79666.png' | relative_url }} "How to use Animaze and Voicemod"){: width="356" height="436"}

Now, when you trigger the Special Action on the Q keybind it will also trigger the Voicemod sound bite.  
This method also works for Animaze Emotes, just use the appropriate keybinds.

## Save a Voicemod Voice Effect to a Quickscene

Animaze can only saves the [Voicemod](https://animaze.tv/voicemod)**Voice Effect in a Quickscene**, so you’ll have to have the **effect selected before saving a Quickscene**.  
When saving a Quickscene, the Voicemod effect isn’t saved by default, you have to scroll down in the Save menu and locate the Save Voicemod Voice Effect and check it

![]({{ '/assets/images/image-20221019-073336-ecef90fb.png' | relative_url }}){: width="331" height="657"}

Next time you load the Quickscene you’ll also change the Voicemod Voice Effect.
