# Importing Ready Player Me avatars

In the Animaze app, you can stream and video chat as your Ready Player Me character. Go from a selfie to streaming as your avatar self in just a couple of clicks:

1. First, go to <https://animaze.readyplayer.me/> and hit the Create A New Avatar button

![Importing Ready Player Me avatars in Animaze by Facerig]({{ '/assets/images/pasted_image_0-c80e5711.png' | relative_url }} "Animaze Ready Player Me avatars"){: width="388" height="587"}

2. Create your avatar from scratch from Ready Player Me presets or from a picture of yourself. You can also customize your avatarfurther with different outfits and looks, so set aside some time to find your right style!

![Importing Ready Player Me avatars in Animaze by Facerig]({{ '/assets/images/pasted_image_0_1_-038b5ef2.png' | relative_url }} "Animaze Ready Player Me avatars"){: width="800" height="862"}

3. When you're ready, click Done. Next, click Copy and paste the link in the web browser's address bar to download your avatar as a GLB file.

![Importing Ready Player Me avatars in Animaze by Facerig]({{ '/assets/images/pasted_image_0_2_-cd0fcc55.png' | relative_url }} " Animaze Ready Player Me "){: width="800" height="867"}

4. Open Animaze. Click the Avatar icon in the left menu bar. Go to My Avatars and click on the \+ sign.

![Importing Ready Player Me avatars in Animaze by Facerig]({{ '/assets/images/pasted_image_0_3_-08904d68.png' | relative_url }} "Animaze Ready Player Me avatars "){: width="800" height="712"}

Pro tip: Remember you can always customize your Ready Player Me avatar in the Animaze Editor, or the Animaze app using the re-texturing feature. Learn more about the retexturing tool [in this specialized article]({{ '/faq/animazedesktop/avatartexture/' | relative_url }})! 

Learn how to set up your stream in [our dedicated article]({{ '/faq/animazedesktop/streamwithanimaze/' | relative_url }})!
