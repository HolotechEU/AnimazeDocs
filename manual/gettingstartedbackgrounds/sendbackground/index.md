# Send a background .avatar file to Animaze Avatar iOS app (on Windows, via Animaze Editor)

The .avatar file format acts like a container which can hold multiple types of avatar content, such as 3D/2D avatars, props, backgrounds.

Follow these steps for the import process:

1. First, make sure that the iphone and the computer are on the same local area network (same LAN/WiFi)

2. There are two ways to enable local connect:

- Go to the **Tools** menu in Animaze Editor and select **Local connect**  from the dropdown menu.

![]({{ '/assets/images/1-82683e4a.png' | relative_url }}){: width="800" height="427"}

- Click the **Connect** button in the upper right corner of the Animaze Editor to connect

![]({{ '/assets/images/2-9d67eb48.png' | relative_url }}){: width="800" height="427"}

3. **Animaze Connect** window will appear, click on the **Start** button - a **QR code** will appear.

![]({{ '/assets/images/3-e992fd32.png' | relative_url }}){: width="800" height="424"}

![]({{ '/assets/images/4-50a7f717.png' | relative_url }}){: width="800" height="423"}

4. Now, open the Animaze Avatar application on your mobile device. Click on the **Settings** **whee**l in the upper right corner of the main screen.

![]({{ '/assets/images/5-d0a01b5c.png' | relative_url }}){: width="250" height="542"}

5. Scroll down to **Animaze** **Editor** and click **Connect**

![]({{ '/assets/images/6-6d5b4916.png' | relative_url }}){: width="250" height="542"}

6. The rear camera is now open to point at the **QR code** and scan it

![]({{ '/assets/images/7-ad990158.PNG' | relative_url }}){: width="250" height="542"}

7. A confirmation of the connection will be received on your phone app (left image) and on the Animaze Editor (right image).

![]({{ '/assets/images/8a-21568f15.png' | relative_url }}){: width="200" height="433"}         ![]({{ '/assets/images/9-f1fff534.png' | relative_url }}){: width="700" height="370"}

Now that you’ve connected the devices together, the next steps send the avatar to your mobile:

8. Go to the **Hierarchy Panel**, right click on the avatar and choose **Send to device**. This sends the avatar to your device, bundled in a **\\*.avatar** file format

![]({{ '/assets/images/10-c7812521.png' | relative_url }}){: width="800" height="424"}

9. A pop-up will be appear on your mobile asking you to accept the file transfer

![]({{ '/assets/images/11-6f91a7ca.png' | relative_url }}){: width="250" height="542"}

10. A confirmation message of the transfer status will be displayed on both devices after you accept

![]({{ '/assets/images/12-92a9f47b.PNG' | relative_url }}){: width="250" height="542"}

11. Open the **Backgrounds** gallery in app and find the background you just sent.

![]({{ '/assets/images/13-38df0f1e.png' | relative_url }}){: width="250" height="542"}      ![]({{ '/assets/images/14-cbfee392.PNG' | relative_url }}){: width="250" height="542"}
