# Import an \\*.avatar bundle in Animaze PC app

Any major bundled item type (3D/2D/Hybrid avatars, backgrounds, props) can be imported and ready to use in Animaze PC app using one of the two import methods: Drag&Drop or Import Custom Avatar feature.

**Drag&Drop**

The simplest way to get your avatar in Animaze Windows app is to click and drag the **\\*.avatar** bundle in the app.

Drag and drop the **.avatar** file anywhere in the app. The system will display it in the Scene once the import is complete.

![]({{ '/assets/images/importbundlehiyori-1607879e.gif' | relative_url }}){: width="1000" height="519"}

**Import Custom Avatar**

1. In the upper left Menu click on the avatar icon, go to the **Imports** tab and click on the **Import Custom Avatars** box.

![]({{ '/assets/images/1-91353b7c.png' | relative_url }}){: width="800" height="532"}

2. Browse for the **\\*.avatar** file on your PC and hit Open.

![]({{ '/assets/images/2-d7b9d829.png' | relative_url }}){: width="700" height="430"}

3. The new avatar is displayed in the Scene.

![]({{ '/assets/images/3-cb7109fd.png' | relative_url }}){: width="992" height="643"}
