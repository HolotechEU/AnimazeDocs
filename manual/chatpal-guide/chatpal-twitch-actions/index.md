## Intro

Enhance your Twitch streams with an AI Sidekick! Let Animaze ChatPal take over, with help of the Twitch Actions feature you can have your avatar react and interact with your Twitch Chat. Additionally, the avatar can cycle randomly through a set o prompts, making the stream more enjoyable and getting rid of the awkward long silent periods.

If the Twitch Actions functionality is new to you, please follow this [guide]({{ '/faq/tech/twitch/' | relative_url }} "https://www.animaze.us/faq/tech/twitch").

We’ve added a new Animaze ChatPal action in the Twitch Action functionality, that enables a couple of options:

1. sending the twitch chat line *directly* to the Animaze ChatPal, which makes the AI Sidekick respond directly to the twitch viewer. Of course, this happens if and when the trigger condition is satisfied.
2. for subs, hosts, raids and chat participation trigger conditions, you can set a custom prompt that is sent to the AI Sidekick (e.g. for every sub the prompt could be “tell me a joke about tomatoes“).

## Usage Guide

In this quick guide we’ll add two twitch actions, one that sends the twitch chat line directly to Animaze ChatPal, and one that sends a custom prompt.

### 

---

### Send chat line directly to Animaze ChatPal

1. Open Animaze.
2. Navigate to the Twitch Actions drawer (click the button on the left-hand side menu).
3. Add a Twitch Action by clicking the “Create a twitch action“.
4. Select “A word said in chat“ from the Choose Twitch Action drop-down menu.
5. Type in “!chatpal“ in the Type a word text box. This will be our trigger word, that will enable sending the chat line to Animaze ChatPal. Click Next when you are done.
6. Now, in the section “Make my avatar do this” navigate to and click the chevron of the “Animaze ChatPal“ option.
7. Now toggle on the Send chat line to Animaze ChatPal and Remove trigger words from prompt.
8. Click Save.
9. Click Next .
10. Click Save.
11. Now you’re all set up with Animaze ChatPal reacting to twitch chat.

### 

---

### Send a custom prompt to Animaze ChatPal

1. Open Animaze.
2. Navigate to the Twitch Actions drawer (click the button on the left-hand side menu).
3. Add a Twitch Action by clicking the “Create a twitch action“.
4. Select “Host“ from the Choose Twitch Action drop-down menu. This works with other triggers like subs, raids, gift subs and chat participation, for simplicity we’ve chosen the list configurable one.
5. Click Next.
6. Now, in the section “Make my avatar do this” navigate to and click the chevron of the “Animaze ChatPal“ option.
7. In the Animaze ChatPal screen, you can now type in a custom prompt, use “tell me a joke“.
8. Click Save.
9. Click Next.
10. Click Save.
11. Now you’re all set up with Animaze ChatPal, the AI Sidekick will get the prompt “tell me a joke“ when you get hosted.
