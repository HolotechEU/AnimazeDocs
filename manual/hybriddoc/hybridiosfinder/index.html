Send your hybrid avatar to Animaze Avatar iOS app (on Mac, via Finder)

The .avatar file format acts like a container which can hold multiple types of avatar content, such as 3D/2D avatars, props, backgrounds.

Follow these steps to import:

  1. Connect the phone to you Mac

  2. Go to finder and select your phone

  1. Once your phone is selected, select Files

 

  1. Select Animaze

  1. Drag and drop your .avatar file from the location you have it stored in the Animaze app window and hit the Sync button 

  1. Restart the mobile app, you will find the sample avatar in the avatar gallery
