# Send your hybrid avatar to Animaze Avatar iOS app (on Windows, via Animaze Editor)

The .avatar file format acts like a container which can hold multiple types of avatar content, such as 3D/2D avatars, props, backgrounds.

To send your avatar to the app, follow the steps bellow:

1. First, make sure that the iphone and the computer are on the same local area network (same LAN/WiFi)

2. There are two ways to enable local connect:

- Go to the Tools menu in Animaze Editor and select Local connect  from the dropdown menu

![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/TgrHNz_aMNTSMZWB5YRPlE8RRVJC2qk6dLCqtdo2cEfR14IkxkGthkxIjwonwMChhWJM3dYLvV9Q_PP0IO5MDGRoSwaVxu4cHKd5nM2I0qNUnVk-YApyiEYX1b_3H8ElBglmzsxH){: width="758" height="405"}

- Click the Connect button in the upper right corner of the Animaze Editor to connect

![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/f-7et5ia0Qx5nG7sNX8JwDWhA1MXh5i-BJVDNo4YsDcoEegWOl1OhzbFtsRcBNyknjAUplDVCROuepjYqy9Y2cUgiXjU0mTTQoPJ89GrmMKROY4Fe4fKtaCcAaOxxZnM0hMG9Bx7){: width="758" height="404"}

3. Animaze Connect window will appear, click on the Start button - a QR code will appear

![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/VR8-8DiDyHOFY6HgeQgaSrJrJYih5hpzifIBQmYk0n4xnL-WgKk2Kva49s5xaboxIuiQMjCEtl3W22Q8kIWoi3q01tMdZbISCWVAUDcv6hKuYBUxTV8ha3J8DZSxGJiO7TROqKeG){: width="758" height="401"}

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/eSBwUpNs63ErssZdC1hSajRczXHSHYQCDYz0UNywvEORoDHICtS4vKnQMXHx4mL61ZFfnKwTzduSbUvVhzC8PshhF2wGGg2MsdmJYOfFbzH-QOcBfF279dTb6qygUDivPJSym0xD){: width="758" height="407"}

4. Now, open the Animaze Avatar application on your mobile device. Click on the Settings wheel in the upper right corner of the main screen

![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/2LgJUSK-uE0kU4zDWsigMulmebz3WoObFJxkshMA_cF03T0aCt9UXQFJMG2lmxGJspgrYC95ZdNJMCu15QrTpfcIWB-xNI_rah5zLmARHbQzagsLHuHM_UMxL5R0cVnIhmjRJs0P){: width="196" height="423"}

5. Scroll down to Animaze Editor and click Connect

![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/RuCa-8g29oxQetH9af3xyVjYfDryNR7fbi7a_u0ORUpYP2m5sd7JmYbMgs0MV_MreMuUWMk-pEXx-Fg3eQ25OFTJc67-fd9ZYbSZUBz8PxNZqq7nNs5qYGaz5VD4vvZkGZWgyPqC){: width="193" height="418"}

6. The rear camera is now open to point at the QR code and scan it

![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/sQloO5DoAzlbo23MlixGI3FRU4KUlcOO70RMw33FTIMZ-fr7mqkLpKCm775pkQtZFjVIJ2hv47nnRzsxRuMqlVArdycz-VvmCbMIP5wI-i3GlTnFfKjdovdzruCkNKsiJ6kd4Xus){: width="194" height="421"}

7. A confirmation of the connection will be received on your phone app (left image) and on the Animaze Editor (right image).

![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/svGvj0E8fszXbBCPOpfX45DZ1I_MoWHo2AxOcwvHPMQrZvzF2Ihw-O8by8y4qVihCVwZLjksagXvyneH_vZKCBn5o0LsMr-w71_4_h_VDgzrGa4LKBHKfHTO5l6YWrE2cb0eofPZ){: width="190" height="411"}![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/o8wRZJFvazQ0f2u_WTPmpjeJVyTLXwTD5quXRrXhKEv-FLD2EynThAGC7JmYtfaJnHo4zVm2xzDJoQFKDJSkzWoneWn9IWn3Ni7gmFxofBCeveh9g_b7OWflxX5aMH807V3oTJ4m){: width="535" height="286"}

Now that you’ve connected the devices together, the next steps send the avatar to your mobile:

8. Go to the Hierarchy Panel, right click on the avatar and choose Send to device. This sends the avatar to your device, bundled in a \\*.avatar file format

![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/8Yih-2xp799PjncbQX7tSgrpZfQYfFFraub2fEivNPG7BFCwyB9V-4tnZ14gDpSWOjbfPVAgjRWHkuFblckkB4eTQM2LpuGobD95zPht0DYNB7v0HC3wsGQvGi9Ws7_Vv0FVZIWa){: width="758" height="403"}

9. A pop-up will be appear on your mobile asking you to accept the file transfer

![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/cS1kUYPoSJkqxL0BbhHYYgehdL0ZiHWNJ-9ohZw_JAWChTkH1Yt_a7QM1P0u9EZ8eWryR2-zYnUJJFuAIfdRx57pvfo7CqieUEoDXCMm3O7cp7u-i3IpZ5CFKNzcwropD1-rgeaU){: width="185" height="399"}

10. A confirmation message of the transfer status will be displayed on both devices after you accept

![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/EXVPiZ1kqgBPswlgC2Fpor9cSil0pfTdW8QyW1SH8CzM7Xv6c9xgsW-BmBV7xi82zM9k_woka5pkfSCPm3GSDJDdSeTS82ghcAqTv_9KvxZbNrw24ZiVXFWgwb3x2Y7LS-0xZl0o){: width="185" height="400"}

11. Open the Avatar gallery in app and find the avatar you just sent

![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/f6gSgTLs-wezate3YnXV3iDboBVwv4lXN1yAiktYLPqqHhcrkYmsPkJoCg7e72J23uAPwXyP7leBYf7agymm5qu86BJESqw6lduWgTYPfjbBTJ8latvN0dB5uHwKv6Mww9uQQDj6){: width="189" height="410"}

![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/0OljkzzR5NwWBZsKTi-6bZ508FReL1WzKwv7gV7waCO7rNwFA3L44H9WHLrFvkb-5-pdralHTvilMWXBo2klfSGHeQoHqKvkAMYx4S3vhutka-AQ9M5wIdCAU9xqFyffuyspjCau){: width="188" height="406"}
