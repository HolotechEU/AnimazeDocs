Modify Proportions

A series of animations that alter the proportions/size of the avatar is available. For example, the avatar can change the size of the ears, eyes, nose, hands etc. by scaling.

They must be added in the "Animations" folder, i.e. an animation can be named “earDeformation”.

Animations will be done differently, depending on the animation method you want to use: skeletal or skeletal + blendshapes.
