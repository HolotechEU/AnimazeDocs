Camera

In the viewport, you see the scene like through a camera lens and you can change the view angle (i.e., zoom, pan) by moving the mouse in combination with the left mouse button.

There are 3 camera modes easy to control, and they are detailed below:

Follow camera

Hold down the left mouse button in the viewport and drag around to move the perspective smoothly, around the item.

Up/down arrows of the keyboard will rotate the camera vertically around the item in the scene.

Left/Right arrows of the keyboard will rotate the camera horizontally around the item in the scene.

Free Camera

This Camera can be used with Alt key and mouse buttons:

Alt + LMB will let you orbit around the item in the scene.

Alt + MMB will let you move within the AnimazeEditor interface.

Alt + RMB will let you zoom in/out the scene.

Fly Camera

Hold down the left mouse button in the viewport and drag around to move the perspective frame by frame, around the item.

Up/down arrows of the keyboard will rotate the camera vertically around the item in the scene.

Left/Right arrows of the keyboard will rotate the camera horizontally around the item in the scene.


More information on the Camera Settings you can use can be found in the specialised Animaze Editor documentation.
